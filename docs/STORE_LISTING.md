## Full Description (4000 characters max)

**Discover Your Next Favorite Movie**

MovieHub is your ultimate companion for exploring the world of cinema. Browse thousands of popular movies, create your personal watchlist, and join a community of movie enthusiasts sharing their thoughts and reviews.

**KEY FEATURES**

**Movie Discovery**
• Browse trending and popular movies
• View detailed information: cast, crew, synopsis, ratings
• Get personalized movie recommendations
• Beautiful movie posters and images

**Powerful Search**
• Search thousands of movies instantly
• Fast and responsive search results
• Discover hidden gems and classics

**Personal Watchlist**
• Save movies you want to watch later
• Sync across all your devices
• Never forget a movie recommendation again

**Real-time Reviews**
• Share your thoughts on movies
• Read reviews from other movie lovers
• Rate movies from 1 to 5 stars
• See reviews update in real-time

**Offline Support**
• Access your watchlist without internet
• Cached movies available offline
• Seamless experience online or offline

**Beautiful Design**
• Modern Material Design 3 interface
• Smooth animations and transitions
• Dark mode support (follows system setting)
• Intuitive navigation

**Secure & Private**
• Your data is secure with Firebase
• Create your own account
• Your watchlist is private to you
• Safe authentication

**PERFECT FOR**
• Movie enthusiasts and cinephiles
• Anyone looking for their next movie to watch
• Film students and critics
• Social movie watchers who love to share opinions

**POWERED BY TMDB**

All movie data is provided by The Movie Database (TMDB), one of the most comprehensive movie databases in the world.

**FREE TO USE**

MovieHub is completely free with no ads or in-app purchases. Enjoy unlimited movie discovery!

**WE VALUE YOUR FEEDBACK**

Have suggestions or found a bug? Contact us at support@moviehub.com

**PRIVACY & PERMISSIONS**

MovieHub requires:
• Internet access: To fetch movie data and sync your watchlist
• Network state: To detect when you're offline

We respect your privacy. We only collect the minimum data necessary to provide the service. Your email is only used for authentication. Read our full privacy policy at: www.moviehub.com/privacy

**COMPATIBLE DEVICES**

Works on all Android devices running Android 7.0 (Nougat) or higher.
